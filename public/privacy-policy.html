Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

Shadow Arrow Prime Marketplace ("We", "Our", "Us") respects your personal data and privacy rights. To provide a seamless e-commerce experience across India and process WhatsApp OTP authentications, we collect necessary personal information when you register, browse, or place an order.

1. Data We Collect

  • Personal Identity Data: Full Name, Mobile Phone Number (for Meta WhatsApp OTP delivery), Email Address.
  • Logistics Data: Street Address, City, State, 6-digit Pincode for parcel dispatch.
  • Technical Identifiers: IP Address, Browser Type, Device Operating System, and Session Cookies.

2. Purpose & Utilization of Data

Your data is gathered strictly for legitimate business execution:

  • Sending real-time Meta WhatsApp OTP codes for secure user login.
  • Dispatching order confirmations, 3-4 Days Express tracking links, and GST invoices via WhatsApp & SMS.
  • Preventing fraudulent orders and unauthorized account access.

3. Third-Party Data Sharing

We NEVER sell, trade, or rent customer personal data to third-party marketing brokers. Data is shared strictly with operational partners:

  • Meta WhatsApp Cloud API: Solely for delivering one-time passcodes (OTP) and transaction messages.
  • Verified Express Couriers: BlueDart, Delhivery, Shadow Express for parcel delivery.
  • PCI-DSS Payment Gateways: Razorpay for verifying payment transactions securely.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion Requests

Under DPDP and Meta Developer guidelines, users have the right to request deletion of their personal data or WhatsApp authentication logs at any time.
